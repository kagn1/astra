01 / Data methodology

What are we measuring?

The interface displays simulated heart rate, peripheral oxygen saturation, blood pressure and skin temperature. These variables are examples of physiological measurements that may be relevant to astronaut health monitoring.

02 / System limitations

What this prototype does not do.

This demonstration does not connect to NASA, real wearable sensors or medical equipment. It does not diagnose medical conditions. Alerts are generated using illustrative threshold rules, not a validated AI model.
